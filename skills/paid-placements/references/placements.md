# Paid placements base

The author's base of paid publication services. Checked 2026-10-29 from the services' own pages unless marked. Prices change — the `placement-scout` agent re-checks before any recommendation. Rumer adds services here; the user's own results go into their platform base.

**Two kinds, two jobs:**
- **Press-release wires** — a release on many news sites at once, fast indexing, Google News on some, the brand on known media. A page that can rank for a branded or news query within days. **Links are usually nofollow** (EIN Presswire states it; Google asks that links in releases be nofollow) — buy them for the ranking page and credibility, not for link equity.
- **Sponsored articles / guest posts** on media sites, through marketplaces or directly — one article on one site you choose. **Dofollow is a per-site option** on most marketplaces. Google's guidelines ask for paid links to carry `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow`; a dofollow paid link is a link-building decision with risk that the owner makes knowingly. The safe value is still real: a page on a site that ranks for the query, read by its audience, with a CTA to the site.

Paid pages on news sites also sit under Google's site reputation abuse policy: a placement can be devalued or removed if the outlet is penalised. Spread placements, log every one, monitor.

## How to choose (summary)

| Goal | Best fit | Watch |
|---|---|---|
| A page that ranks for a brand or news query fast | A wire with Google News (EIN, ACCESS) or a sponsored article on a site that already ranks in the niche | Wire links are nofollow; the niche must be allowed |
| A dofollow link to a product page | Marketplace site with dofollow, DR 40+, real search traffic in the target country, topically close | Paid-link risk; vary anchors; check the link's rel after publishing |
| Credibility ("as seen on") | Top wires (GlobeNewswire, PR Newswire, Business Wire, ACCESS Premium) or a known outlet on a marketplace | Price on request — ask for the outlet list first |
| Referral traffic | A sponsored article on a site whose audience matches, with a strong CTA and UTM | Estimated views on the marketplace card are estimates |
| Nutra / supplements | Not EIN (bans supplements); check each wire's rules; marketplace sites in health that accept it | Health claims only as approved |
