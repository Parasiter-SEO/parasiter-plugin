# Forum experience mode — threads of a forum cluster (Rumer's method №68)

Used when the brief comes from the forum cluster (`../../content-type-selector/references/forum-cluster.md`) or the user asks for "a forum post from my experience", "a thread like a real one", "write the cluster". It replaces the article template: a forum thread is a person sharing what happened to them, not an article. Everything else in this skill still holds — facts, links, honesty, the user's own links, the check before handing over.

## 1. The interview — before the first thread of a cluster

One message, at most 6 short questions, skip what the brief or the profile answers:

1. **Did you (or someone close who agreed to share) really use it?** How long, which package, why you started.
2. **What happened** — week by week if you remember: first days, the first change, the results with numbers (kg, hours of sleep, $ earned, days to delivery…).
3. **What didn't go well** — side effects, delivery, taste, price, a refund, what disappointed you.
4. **What surprised you**, and one small concrete detail only a real user knows (the taste, the box, the support email, the app screen).
5. **Would you buy it again, and who is it NOT for?**
6. **Photos or screenshots** you can add (the box, the order, a chart), and the forum's rules on links if you know them.

The answers are saved under `experience:` for this product in `parasite-seo-writer.md`, so the other threads of the cluster reuse them and never ask again.

**No real use → the research angle, still first person and personal:** "I spent a week comparing X and Y before buying", "I read 200 buyer complaints so you don't have to", "I asked my pharmacist about X". True story of the research, true numbers from it. Never invented weeks of use, results, weight lost, before/after or photos — say this in one line once, without lecturing, and go on with the research angle.

## 2. How a thread sounds (native, not an article)

- **First person, plain talk**, the way people write on that forum: short paragraphs, everyday words, a bit of hesitation and opinion ("honestly", "not gonna lie", "your mileage may vary" — only where the forum talks like that). Match the forum's language and spelling (US / UK).
- **No article look:** no H1/H2 headings, no table of contents, no "In this article", no "quick facts box", no marketing words ("revolutionary", "game-changer", "act now"). At most bold labels like **Week 1:** or a short list where a person would make one. One small table only when the story is a comparison.
- **Concrete details beat adjectives:** dates, numbers, the package, what it cost, what support answered.
- **Downsides in every thread.** A thread with no "but" reads like an ad and gets reported.
- **End with a question to the forum** ("Anyone else tried it past 3 months?", "Did you get the same stomach thing in week 1?") — threads with replies stay alive in "Discussions and forums".
- **Link:** where the forum allows it, once, in the place a person would put it ("this is the one I ordered: …"), with the user's affiliate link and a short honest disclosure where the forum or the law needs it ("affiliate link, same price for you"). Forum forbids it → no link in the post; the link goes into the profile or signature if allowed, and the operator checklist says so.

## 3. Thread structure (adapt to the angle)

1. **Title** — a person's title with the query words: "Ikaria juice after 8 weeks — my honest results (with numbers)". Unique across the cluster and on the forum; never a competitor's title.
2. **Who I am and why I tried it** — 2–4 lines: age or situation, the problem, what I tried before.
3. **What I did** — the product, the package, the price paid, how I used it.
4. **What happened** — the timeline with numbers, the good and the bad.
5. **My verdict** — worth it or not, for whom, who should skip it, what I'd do differently.
6. **The link** (if allowed) and the question to the forum.

## 4. The cluster: every thread is different

For a cluster of 3–5 threads on one query, each thread takes its angle from the plan (my results · a question I answer · comparison from use · mistakes · for a specific person · doubts and downsides · update). Different title, different opening line, different order and length. The same real facts may repeat; the words, structure and angle may not. Before handing over, compare the threads with each other: if two openings or structures look alike, rewrite one.

**Length:** the main story thread 1,200–1,800 words; the other angles 500–1,000 words, as real threads are. If the user asked for a number of words, reach at least that number in every thread.

**The author's follow-ups:** with each thread, write 2–3 short replies the author can post **in their own name** later in that thread (a real update after 2–4 weeks, an answer to a likely question from People also ask). Never replies from other "users".

## 5. Deliver

Per thread: the title, the post as plain forum text in the chat (ready to paste; BBCode only on request), the forum and the day from the cluster plan, the follow-ups, the operator checklist (facts to verify, link rule of this forum, photos to add). The `.docx` with all threads of the batch in one file. Mark each thread `written` in the profile's `forum_clusters:`. Next steps: "write the next thread", "I published", "recheck the forum top in 7 days".
