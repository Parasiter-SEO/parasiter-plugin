# Page score — two scores, then the total

Every audit gives **two scores out of 100** and their average as the page score:

1. **Rumer's rules** — does the page follow the author's working rules (what sales-review-writer builds)?
2. **Copywriting** — does it work as selling copy for a buyer: useful, problem → solution, motivates the click and the purchase?

A page can follow every rule and still read flat, or read well and miss the rules; showing both tells the user what kind of fix it needs.

## 1. Rumer's rules (0–100)

| Rule | Points |
|---|---|
| **Photo at the top** — a real or custom image (product in hand, author, comparison card), not stock, right under the title: it is what Google tends to take for the snippet | 10 |
| **Facts box for the main product** — up to 10 lines: price, quality, my rating x/10, special offers with checked date, guarantee, best for, key parameters, then the link | 15 |
| **Author** — who, role, why trust, why listen; real, not no-name | 10 |
| **Pains** — 3–5 concrete pains in the buyer's words | 10 |
| **Facts → how the product solves each pain** | 10 |
| **Life after** — concrete everyday picture, no guarantees | 5 |
| **Prices and current offers block** — every package, best value, deal, checked date, CTA to the package page | 10 |
| **Comparison with 2–3 competitors + honest alternatives** | 10 |
| **TOC with telling headings** | 5 |
| **1,500+ words** (article formats; platform minimum for others) | 10 |
| **Product name linked about once per section; links land on the right page** | 5 |

## 2. Copywriting (0–100)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| **Snippet** — the title promises a clear value and a reason to click after the AI answer; the page delivers it 100% | 10 |
| **Problem → solution** — the page opens the reader's problem, makes it felt, and solves it with the product step by step (PAS / AIDA logic), each claim tied to a pain | 20 |
| **Usefulness** — the reader learns something they can use even if they don't buy: how to choose, what to avoid, which package, who it is not for | 15 |
| **Motivation to click and buy** — reasons why this product, why this offer, why now (from real conditions); objections and doubts answered before the CTA | 15 |
| **CTAs** — at the decision points, specific action text, the best landing page; not "click here" | 10 |
| **Images that support the text** — 3–6: facts card, pains → solution, comparison, prices, verdict; each next to the text it supports, facts and dates correct | 10 |
| **Trust and honesty** — cons, who it's not for, disclosure, last-checked date, no invented tests / reviews / results / deadlines | 10 |
| **Readability and platform fit** — short paragraphs, lists, tables that fit on mobile, the platform's tone and link rules | 10 |

## 1b. Brand rules (agency role, replaces score 1 for client pages)

| Rule | Points |
|---|---|
| **Photo at the top** — the client's real product or a realistic cover in the brand's style, not stock | 10 |
| **Brand facts box** — what it is, price from / plans, who it is for, what makes it different, trial or guarantee, link; facts from the brief or the client's site | 10 |
| **Who is behind the page** — the brand's real expert or team, role shown, plus the brand disclosure ("Written by the Acme team" / "Prepared with Acme") | 10 |
| **Pains → facts → how the product solves them**, life after (no guarantees) | 15 |
| **Comparison and honest alternatives** — competitors described fairly; the brand not crowned by a fake test | 10 |
| **Prices / plans block** where the client publishes prices | 5 |
| **Brand voice and approved claims only** — tone and words from the brief; regulated claims only as approved | 10 |
| **Looks great** — consistent design: images in the client's colours and fonts, infographics with exact facts, clean formatting, tables that fit on mobile — the page the client is proud to show | 10 |
| **Links** — UTM on every link to the client (`utm_medium=parasite`, right source and content), landing on the brief's destination, product name linked about once per section | 10 |
| **TOC with telling headings; 1,500+ words** for article formats | 10 |

## Caps

- Each critical issue caps its line at half its points.
- Any fabricated claim (fake review, test, result, deadline, "my experience" the user doesn't have) caps **both** scores at 50 until removed.
- No affiliate disclosure → Copywriting capped at 70. Client pages: no brand disclosure → both scores capped at 70.
- Client pages: the client's product presented as an independent review, test or ranking → both scores capped at 50.

## Reading the total

| Total | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 85–100 | Ready; test and track |
| 70–84 | Good; fix the medium items |
| 50–69 | Losing sales; fix critical items first |
| 0–49 | Rewrite with sales-review-writer |

The same scale is used by `work-log` (audit under 70 → back in the queue). Client pages go to the client at 75 or more.
