# Page score (0–100)

| Area | Points |
|---|---|
| Snippet (title, keyword, reason to click) | 10 |
| First screen (quick facts + deal + CTA + image) | 20 |
| Author and hook | 15 |
| TOC and headings | 10 |
| Structure for the content type | 15 |
| CTAs and offer | 15 |
| Honesty and disclosure | 10 |
| Platform fit and readability | 5 |

Each critical issue caps its area at half its points. Any fabricated claim (fake review, test, result, deadline) caps the total at 50 until removed.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 85–100 | Ready; test and track |
| 70–84 | Good; fix the medium items |
| 50–69 | Losing sales; fix critical items first |
| 0–49 | Rewrite with sales-review-writer |
