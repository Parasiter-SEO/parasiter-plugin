## Rank above competitors on forums (forum cluster)
Say "forum cluster for <query>" (or "how do I beat my competitors' forum threads?"). Rumer's method: PARASITER reads Google's "Discussions and forums" block and the forum threads in the top 20 for your queries, with their age (your own SerpApi free searches first, DataForSEO with the cost shown first, web search without either), and gives each query a verdict: OPEN (old top, post now), WINDOW, CROWDED or NO FORUMS. It takes apart the competitor threads that won (title, opening, story, replies, forum rules) to find the pattern, never to copy it. Then it plans 3–5 threads per query, each with its own angle (my results, a question I answer, comparison from use, mistakes, for a specific person, downsides, update), on different forums, 1–2 a day. A short interview about your real experience comes first, and every thread is written as a native first-person post with downsides and a question to the forum. Your link goes only where the forum allows it. No real use means the research angle ("I compared…"), never invented results. Fresh threads push the old ones out, and several threads on one query take more places in the block. In 5–10 days say "recheck the forum top" to see which of your threads got in.

## Your plan for the day as a page
Say "plan for today". The plan opens as a page in PARASITER's style: goal and week progress, task cards with time, what each moves, a phrase to copy and a "done" checkbox. In the Claude app it is an artifact; in Claude Code it is `plan-today.html` in your PARASITER folder, opened in the browser (older days: `plans/`). The same short list is in the chat. "Am I on track?" gives the weekly review the same way.

## Get a free key (Free)
On parasiter.affiliatecredo.com/free.html enter your email (no card). The Free key comes by email within a minute (it starts with `psr_`; one new key per address a day). It opens a snapshot of 50 checked platforms ("show the free platform base") and 3 platform scans a week, one product each ("where do reviews of <product> rank?") — the top 3 platforms in full, the rest as a count. Connect it like any key (next section). "How many scans do I have left?" shows the week. The key also opens the **Free journal**: up to 10 pages — say "published: <link>" after you publish, "are my pages alive?" to check them, "is my page indexed?" (your own SerpApi free plan or DataForSEO). Sales imports, reports, the publication queue and Telegram alerts are Pro.

## Connect your key (Free, Pro, Insider)
The Pro / Insider key comes in the welcome email after payment, the Free key in the Free email (both start with `psr_`). It links the plugin to the PARASITER server: checked platform database and feed, news, weekly analysis, daily page checks, Telegram. Lost the steps? The bot sends them again: /setup.
1. Open Claude Code and type `/plugin marketplace add Parasiter-SEO/parasiter-plugin`, then `/plugin install parasiter@parasiter`.
2. During install the plugin asks for "PARASITER key (Free, Pro or Insider)": paste the key. No key yet: leave it empty — the content skills work, platforms need at least the Free key.
3. Check: say "my PARASITER plan" → `account_info` shows the plan and until when.
Need to change the key? Reinstall the plugin and paste the key when asked. Never paste the key into the chat.

## Get news and notifications in Telegram (Pro, Insider)
Say "connect Telegram" → open the personal link → press Start. The bot sends invites to your plan's channel (and the Insider group). "What's new?" shows the same news in Claude.
After linking the bot pins a start guide with every command and sends it as a designed PDF, plus a web version with phrase search and tap-to-copy (both linked in the welcome email: parasiter.affiliatecredo.com/guides/pro-en.html, insider-en.html, -uk for Ukrainian); `/help` sends both again. Bot commands: `/help` start guide · `/status` your plan and until when · `/skills` what you can say to PARASITER (the same list as in the welcome email) · `/invites` new invites to your chats · `/setup` how to install the plugin and connect the key · `/checkin on|off` Monday check-in · `/upgrade` Pro → Insider, pay only the difference · `/support`, `/bug`, `/idea`, `/feedback` write to Rumer.
