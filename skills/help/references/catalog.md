PARASITER by Rumer — parasite SEO inside the user's AI assistant, built for **affiliate marketers at any stage** (review pages on trusted platforms → affiliate link → commission): Rumer's method, the checked platform base, the journal of pages and sales, the daily plan. Built for affiliates only: no roles, no agency or client modes. Skills hold the method; agents do research and checks in their own context and hand back facts with sources. The user never calls agents; skills send them — at once on Claude Max / Team, on Claude Pro as the full version when the user says "full" (otherwise the skill's Lite version: same method, smaller scope; see `navigator/references/agents-and-plan.md`).

## The workflow in 5 steps

The user-facing overview of every skill and agent is the capability map: `navigator/references/capability-map.md` (shown at first start, every session, and on "what can PARASITER do?").

1. **Plan** — `navigator` (today's plan) and `goals` (baseline, goals, weekly review).
2. **Find** — `affiliate-finder` (what to promote), `niche-analysis` (keywords and SERPs), `platform-finder` / `platform-feed` (where to publish), `platform-analyzer` (is this platform worth it).
3. **Write** — `content-type-selector` (what to write), `sales-review-writer` (the article), `post-illustrations` (images), `page-audit` (check before publishing).
4. **Publish, index and log** — the user publishes; `work-log` records the page and queues checks; `indexing` gets it into Google (your own site in Search Console if you have one, otherwise the indexer you chose) and counts what works; `platform-monitor` checks alive / indexed.
5. **Measure** — `work-log` (sales, traffic, reports, what earns), `client-reports` (what earns and where to scale), back to `goals` for the weekly review.

## Rumer's terms: Web 2.0, Web 3.0, Creative SEO

Rumer's own terms, not the usual meanings ("Web 2.0" elsewhere means any user-published site, "Web3" means blockchain). Full definitions: `rumer-concepts.md`.

| Term | Rumer's definition | Where in PARASITER |
|---|---|---|
| **proven platform** | Affiliate reviews already rank there now; everyone can see it and post. | platform-finder (base, live scan, "follow" track), platform-analyzer |
| **Web 2.0 🔒** | A platform other SEOs barely use. You are its discoverer: only you know it and work it, and you keep it hidden from other SEOs in your personal platform list. | platform-finder pattern search ("clean" track), platform-analyzer (Web 2.0 candidate), work-log (`class web2`: shown apart, never sent anywhere, left out of the Telegram summary) |
| **Web 3.0** | A platform everyone sees: its pages are in the top, but nobody knows how to post there, because the whole process is the barrier. Rumer's example is Amazon S3 (AWS). | platform-finder (the "Web 3.0" block), platform-analyzer Web 3.0 mode ("how do people publish on X?"), work-log (`class web3`) |
| **Creative SEO** | Rumer's main principle: invent non-standard, original ideas in parasite SEO that give new strategies, methods and tools, either still poorly explored or completely new. PARASITER itself is a result of Creative SEO, a tool that didn't exist before. | Web 2.0 search and Web 3.0 research; "give me a Creative SEO idea" in any skill; offered when the standard route stalls (empty search, saturated niche, platform cleaned up) |

## What PARASITER does not do

- Does not publish or post anything itself: it prepares pages, the user publishes.
- Does not create accounts, buy links or work with hacked sites, hidden upload forms or fake reviews.
- Does not log in to affiliate networks: the user exports reports and uploads them.
- Does not promise income or rankings; income in plans is "possible income" at your own numbers.
- Never runs a paid check (index, positions, scans on your SEO API) without your yes and the cost shown first.
