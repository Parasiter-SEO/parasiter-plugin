## Are press-release links dofollow?
Usually not: EIN Presswire states nofollow only, and Google asks that links in releases and paid links be nofollow or sponsored. A wire buys a page on news sites that ranks fast and credibility. Dofollow comes from chosen sites on marketplaces (prnews.io, Collaborator, Adsy) — a paid dofollow link is a link-building decision with risk that you make knowingly. PARASITER checks the real rel of links on published pieces.

## What are Web 2.0, Web 3.0 and Creative SEO?
These are Rumer's own terms. They don't mean what they usually mean on the internet.

## Why can't every sale be tied to a page?
Networks often show no sub-ID or referrer, buyers come back later, and many pages promote the same offer. UTM tags don't help: they go to the merchant's analytics, which the affiliate doesn't see. PARASITER counts every sale exactly by program, month and pack; per-page numbers add an estimated share, always labelled. A sub-ID per page (or per platform / pack), separate Amazon tracking IDs and network click reports make it more precise.

## Terms (explain in one line when asked)
- **Offer** — a product an affiliate program pays you to sell.

- **Web 3.0 (Rumer's term)** — a platform everyone sees in the top, but nobody knows how to publish on it; the process is the barrier (example: Amazon S3).
- **Creative SEO (Rumer's term)** — inventing non-standard ideas that give new parasite SEO strategies, methods and tools; Rumer's main principle, and PARASITER itself came out of it.

## The PARASITER server doesn't connect
`/mcp` shows **parasiter** failed with *"InvalidHTTPResponse fetching https://parasiter.affiliatecredo.com"* or *"Auth: not authenticated"*, and "my PARASITER plan" doesn't load. Most often an antivirus with web or HTTPS protection breaks the connection — it checks every program's traffic, not only the browser, so turning off its firewall alone doesn't help.
- **Norton 360:** Norton → Scam Protection → Safe Web → This device → Exclusions → add `parasiter.affiliatecredo.com`.
- **Avast, AVG, Kaspersky, Bitdefender, ESET and others:** add `parasiter.affiliatecredo.com` to the exclusions of web / HTTPS scanning (Web Shield, Safe Browsing, Online Threat Prevention and the like).
- Then in Claude Code: `/mcp` → **plugin:parasiter:parasiter** → Reconnect.
Still failing? Say "my PARASITER plan": if it says the key was not accepted, paste the key from your latest email again (/plugin → PARASITER → configure options). Otherwise tell Rumer via the bot (/bug) with your antivirus name.

## On Windows the DataForSEO or SerpApi server doesn't start
They start with the `python3` command. Python from python.org installs only `python` and `py`, so `/mcp` shows **parasiter-dataforseo** / **parasiter-serpapi** failed. Install Python from the Microsoft Store (it adds `python3`), then restart Claude Code. Python from python.org can stay.
