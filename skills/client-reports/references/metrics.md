# Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue per publication | commission of the page's sub-ID / 1 | Compare pages and platforms |
| EPC | commission / affiliate clicks | Quality of traffic and offer fit |
| Conversion rate | sales / affiliate clicks | Offer page + pre-sell quality |
| CTR (SERP) | clicks / impressions | Snippet quality (own pages, GSC) |
| RPM_SERP | (clicks/impressions) × (sales/clicks) × avg commission × 1000 | Revenue per 1000 search impressions — only with impression data |
| Page age | today − publish date | Put results in context (14/30/60 days) |

Example (arithmetic, not a forecast): 10,000 impressions, CTR 2%, conversion 3%, commission $40 → 6 sales, $240, RPM_SERP $24.

Where third-party platforms hide impressions, compare EPC and revenue per publication instead; never present those as RPM_SERP.
