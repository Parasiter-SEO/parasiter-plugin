# Brand profiles — checklist

Where a brand should have a declared, complete profile, and what each one does for parasite SEO. Pick by audience: the "for" column decides the order.

| Platform | For | Can host pages that rank | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn company page (+ founder / expert profiles) | B2B, services, SaaS, hiring | Yes — LinkedIn articles and long posts by the brand's experts | logo, cover, About with the site link, industry, size, last post; experts list the company |
| YouTube channel | all | Yes — video titles and descriptions rank in Google and video blocks | name, avatar, banner, About + links, playlists, last upload; description links to the site carry UTM (`utm_source=youtube`) |
| Medium (profile or publication) | B2B, tech, finance, health information | Yes — articles | bio, link, publication name, last article |
| Reddit (brand account, disclosed) | B2C, tech, communities | Yes, where the subreddit allows brand participation — answers and posts | username shows the brand, flair if offered, history of useful answers; read each subreddit's rules |
| Quora (brand profile / Space) | B2C, B2B information | Yes — answers rank and appear in "Discussions and forums" | credential states the brand role, Space if active |
| X (Twitter) | tech, media, B2B | Rarely ranks; brand signal | handle, bio, link, pinned post, last post |
| Facebook page | B2C, local | Rarely; brand signal and reviews | category, contacts, link, last post |
| Instagram | B2C, visual products | No (link in bio only); brand signal | bio, link, highlights, last post |
| TikTok | B2C, young audience | Video search inside TikTok, sometimes Google video | bio, link, last video |
| Pinterest | home, fashion, food, DIY, e-commerce | Yes — pins rank in image search | business account, boards by topic, pins link to the site |
| Google Business Profile | local businesses | Maps and local pack | category, hours, photos, real reviews only |
| Crunchbase / G2 / Capterra / Trustpilot / Clutch | SaaS, agencies, services | Listing pages rank for brand + "reviews" | claimed listing, description, link; reviews only from real customers, never seeded |
| Industry directories and associations | by niche | Listing pages | name, link, category |
| Podcast hosts (Spotify, Apple via Audioboom / RSS) | B2B, experts | Episode pages rank | show page, episode notes with links |
| Document / slide hosts (Gamma, SlideShare-like, Pressbooks, Issuu) | guides, research, B2B | Yes — guides and chapters | author shows the brand |

## Consistency (the same everywhere)

- Brand name spelled the same; the same logo and colours.
- One-line description, the same meaning everywhere (adapted to length limits).
- Link to the site (the right page per audience), contact.
- The brand's experts (founder, specialists) are real people with their own profiles linking to the company.

## Score per platform

0 — missing · 1 — exists but empty, broken link or inactive over 6 months · 2 — complete and consistent · 3 — complete, consistent and active (posts in the last 30 days).
