# Offer score (0–20)

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search demand | none found | <100/mo | 100+/mo for product queries |
| Commission | low | medium | strong for the niche |
| Traffic rules | forbids content/SEO or third-party platforms | unclear | explicitly allowed |
| Saturation | pushed by everyone, refund-heavy | moderate | few serious competitors |
| Offer page | lead-capture first / broken / weak | average | clear packages, guarantee, converts |
| Price stability | price cut far below listing | occasional changes | stable |
| Payout threshold | hard to reach | reachable in months | reachable fast / network payout |
| Cookie | ≤1 day | 7–30 days | 30+ days |
| Payment reliability | complaints of non-payment | unknown | good track record |
| Support | none | slow | manager answers, gives creatives/coupons |

15–20 promote · 10–14 test · <10 skip.

## Commission models
- **CPA** — fixed payout per sale/lead.
- **RevShare** — percentage of what the customer pays; recurring for subscriptions/SaaS.
- **Hybrid** — fixed + percentage.

## Questions for the affiliate manager
1. Is SEO/content traffic from third-party platforms (forums, Medium, PDFs, press releases) allowed?
2. Brand-name keywords in titles allowed?
3. Coupons or exclusive deals for affiliates?
4. Real average payout including upsells and refunds?
5. Payout threshold, schedule and methods?
