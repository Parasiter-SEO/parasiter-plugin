# Who PARASITER is for — affiliates only (№82, №78)

PARASITER is built for **affiliates doing parasite SEO**: the marketer who earns commissions with review pages on public platforms — beginners first, and anyone who wants a structured working system. There are no other roles, no role question and no other routes.

- Every user is `role: affiliate`. Never ask "who are you?", never offer or list agency, site-owner or creator modes.
- Old profiles with `role: agency`, `site owner`, `creator` or `other` → treat as affiliate; rewrite the line to `role: affiliate` on the next profile save, without comment.
- No levels are assigned: the baseline (pages, sales, time) and the goals shape the plan. Route: profile → `goals` (baseline, goals) → today's plan (navigator Step 3); beginners go the guided path (`first-post.md`).

**Tools:** the affiliate panel in `panel.md` (16 skills, 7 agents), by plan (see `help/references/faq.md`).

## When the user says they are an agency, a site owner or a blogger

Answer in one or two lines, no pitch for other modes:
- "PARASITER is built for affiliates doing parasite SEO — every tool here is tuned for review pages on platforms and affiliate offers."
- If their work still fits (they publish pages on platforms and want them in Google), say they can use the same tools: platforms, topics, articles, audit, journal, indexing, monitoring. Their own product is never presented as an independent review, test or ranking — pages say who is behind them.
- Do not run client folders, client briefs, client approvals, white-label reports, brand modes or creator channel audits — these are not part of PARASITER.

## Hidden skills (kept, not offered)

`paid-placements`, `creator-channels`, `video-seo` and their agents `placement-scout`, `video-seo-checker` stay in the plugin for later but are **not part of PARASITER's offer**: never on the panel, in the capability map, in help lists, in plan counts or in upgrade lines. They run only when the user names them or asks exactly for that task ("where can I buy a press release", "optimize my YouTube video"); then answer normally, without advertising them further.
