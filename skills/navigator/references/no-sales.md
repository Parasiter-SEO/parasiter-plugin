# "No sales" — the funnel check (№87)

**Triggers:** "no sales", "I don't earn", "it doesn't work", "PARASITER doesn't work", "nothing happens", "waste of money", "why no money", "Google hates me", "nobody buys". Work label `▸ PARASITER · navigator`.

**The principle.** Sales are the result of the user's choices — the offer, the angle, the volume of pages, the platforms, the time put in. PARASITER's part is the intermediate results that lead there: pages live, in the index, in positions, getting traffic and clicks. So the answer to "no sales" is never an apology and never a promise, and never "you failed": it is the user's own numbers, step by step, showing where the chain breaks and which lever is theirs to pull now.

## 1. Read the chain from the journal (and say what's missing)

From `parasite-seo-progress.csv`, `parasite-seo-sales.csv` and the latest checks, in one table:

| Step | Your numbers | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Pages live | N live of M published · on K platforms | Rumer's rule: 10 platforms / 100 live posts |
| In Google's index | N of live | most live pages indexed within days with an indexer |
| Positions | in top 3: a · top 10: b · top 20: c | sales usually start at 10–15 posts in the top 3 |
| Traffic / views | from platform stats, if logged | — |
| Clicks on affiliate links | from the network / shortener, if logged | — |
| Sales | N · commission | — |

A step with no data is **unknown**, not zero: say which export or check would fill it (and offer it — paid checks with the cost first). Time matters too: say how many weeks since the first page; 4–8 weeks without a sale is normal at the start.

## 2. Name the break point and its lever

The first step where the numbers drop is where to work. One break, one main lever, the skill that does it:
- **Few pages live / few platforms** → volume: more pages on more platforms (`platform-finder`, the rollout, today's plan). Most "no sales" at the start is simply too few pages.
- **Pages removed** → the platform choice: move and republish (`platform-monitor`, the publication queue).
- **Not indexed** → indexing: the clean list for an indexer (`indexing`).
- **Indexed, not ranking** → topic and competition: a weaker query, a fresher angle, more posts in the cluster, a refresh (`content-type-selector`, forum cluster, `niche-analysis` on Insider).
- **Ranking, no clicks** → the page: first screen, facts box, CTA (`page-audit`).
- **Clicks, no sales** → the offer: the wrong product, a weak landing page, price, country or the network (`affiliate-finder`; check the network's own stats). Changing the offer is often the fastest fix.

## 3. Say it plainly

Close with 3 lines: what the system has already given (the intermediate results in numbers) · where the chain breaks · the one action for this week, as a ready phrase. Tone: calm, factual, on the user's side — "here is where it breaks and here is the lever", never blame, never "PARASITER isn't working", never "you did it wrong", never a promise of income or a date.

## 4. Did the work and still no sales → Rumer looks at it himself

While `account_info.rumer_sales_review` is true, and the chain shows the user **has done the work** — at least 20 live pages on 3+ platforms, most of them indexed, some in the top 20, 6+ weeks since the first page — and still no sales, add after the 3 lines:

"You've done the work and the chain still doesn't close — that's exactly what Rumer wants to see himself. Send him your numbers in the bot: /support no sales · <pages live> live · <indexed> indexed · top 3/10/20: <a/b/c> · clicks: <n or unknown> · offer: <program / product> · weeks: <n>."

Give that line ready to copy, with the user's numbers filled in. Never page URLs, links or texts in it — numbers, the program and the product only. Don't promise when or whether Rumer answers. `rumer_sales_review` false or missing → skip this part. Not done the work yet → no Rumer line: the lever from §2 is the answer.

If the user asks for a refund: answer honestly (14 days from payment, offer clause 13; after that the payment is final) and still offer the funnel check — never argue.
