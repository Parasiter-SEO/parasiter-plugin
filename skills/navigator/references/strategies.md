### 1 · $100 a day — the 30-day plan (Rumer's YouTube method)
Goal Rumer set for himself: top rankings for reviews of 3 products to reach about $100 a day (his result, not a promise). Plan: ~145 posts across ~45 free platforms in 30 days, about 5 a day.
Steps (Rumer's 8): niche → competition check → 3 products → join the programs → buyer keywords → content → platforms → post optimization; then the funnel, indexing, ranking checks and constant improvement.
Skills: `niche-analysis` (Insider) or the content-type-selector SERP check · `affiliate-finder` (Insider) / `starter_offers` · `content-type-selector` → `sales-review-writer` (one text per platform type, never copies) · `platform-finder` (Free: the 50 base) · `page-audit` · `work-log` (daily count vs 145) · `indexing` · `platform-monitor`.
Rules: unique text per platform, spread across many domains (site reputation abuse risk), platform rules first.

### 2 · Systematic parasite SEO
Rumer's rule: at least 10 platforms and 100 live posts; first sales usually once 10–15 posts reach the top 3 (varies). Every post goes into the journal with its sub-ID; weekly: which posts, platforms, products and formats earn → the publication queue scales winners, fixes near-misses, drops what doesn't move.
Skills: `goals` · `work-log` (journal, queue, reports) · `platform-monitor` · `client-reports` · the navigator's daily plan (Pro).

### 3 · Content that sells (Rumer's SEO Conversion Framework)
Traffic without sales is a content problem. Formats that still get clicks and sales: best-of lists of 10–15, A vs B, personal experience / my results, complaints and doubts, prices and packages, expert review. Structure: the reader's pain → facts → life after → prices → honest comparison → CTA; quick facts box, author block, images that carry facts.
Skills: `content-type-selector` (12-point Rumer filter) · `sales-review-writer` · `post-illustrations` · `page-audit` (80+ to publish).

### 4 · Forum strategy
Look at which forum threads hold the top and Google's "Discussions and forums" block for the query; write 3–5 fresh first-person threads, each its own angle, on different forums, 1–2 a day — fresh threads push the old ones out. Plus joining live threads with 1–2 native replies that answer a real question, one link where it truly answers it.
Skills: `content-type-selector` (forum cluster, joining discussions) · `sales-review-writer` (forum format) · `work-log` (each thread with a sub-ID) · recheck in 5–10 days.

### 5 · Hidden platforms (Web 2.0, Rumer's term)
A platform other SEOs barely use: you discover it, you work it alone, you keep it in your private list. Find candidates by the pattern of platforms that already worked (forum engine, document hosts, site builders, fundraising, events…), test one post, check indexing.
Skills: `platform-finder` pattern mode (Pro / Insider) · `platform-analyzer` · `work-log` (class web2, private) · `platform-monitor` (never shared in summaries).

### 6 · Web 3.0 platforms (Rumer's term)
A domain whose pages rank, visible to everyone, but with no obvious way to post — the process itself is the barrier. Research the legitimate route from the service's own documentation, cost, indexing and risks; only legal, by the service's terms.
Skills: `platform-finder` (Web 3.0 block) · `platform-analyzer` ("how do people publish on X?").

### 7 · Documents, PDF & press
Long-form reviews as PDFs on document hosts and flipbooks, presentation pages, news-style pages and press releases (free where possible; paid wires only for site owners with a budget, marked as paid). Fast indexing, long shelf life when the host is stable.
Skills: `sales-review-writer` (PDF / press release formats) · `platform-finder` (docs, flipbooks) · `paid-placements` (site owners, agencies) · `indexing`.

### 8 · Creative SEO (Rumer's main principle)
When every usual method is crowded, invent a non-standard one: a new platform type, a new format, a new angle on the query. PARASITER itself is a result of Creative SEO.
Skills: `content-type-selector` (Creative SEO topics) · `platform-finder` (empty category → Creative SEO idea) · `platform-analyzer` to test the idea on one post before scaling.
