# Platform score (0–100)

| Criterion | Points | Full points when |
|---|---|---|
| Affiliate reviews ranking there | 40 | Review pages of affiliate products (any niche, ideally the user's) are in the Google top 10 on this domain now |
| Fresh indexing | 30 | New posts / pages indexed within ~48 h |
| Access | 10 | New account can register and post (free) — or a known price and way to buy (paid) |
| Moderation friction | 10 | No pre-moderation, no keyword blacklist |
| Fit | 5 | Format and audience suit the content type and offer |
| Links | 5 | A link to the offer is possible somewhere (body, profile, signature, description). Link indexing and nofollow don't matter — the page being indexed does |

Partial points for partial conditions. A platform with **noindex on new posts scores 0 for indexing and cannot exceed 40 total**.

**Lifespan is not scored and never blocks a platform.** If affiliate reviews on it are in the top 10 now, it goes to work today — even if it may die tomorrow; nobody can predict that. Lifespan only gives the stability label: **stable** (affiliate pages on it have lived 2+ weeks), **new** (under 1–2 weeks or unknown), **unstable** (pages are being removed now).

| Score | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Any score with affiliate reviews in the top 10 now | Publish now — test 1–3 posts today |
| 50+ without reviews in the top 10 | Test 1–3 posts, check in 3–7 days |
| 30–49 | Only if nothing better |
| 0–29 | Skip |

Labels for the summary line: Free / Paid · Affiliate reviews in top 10: yes (N found) / no · Indexing fast/medium/slow/none · Stability stable/new/unstable · Link: in body / profile only / none.
